# Governance and Compliance Boundary

This student-built prototype is for research-navigation and educational evaluation. It is not an official Wake Forest or Duke service, a legal or medical decision tool, or a substitute for librarians, instructors, accessibility staff, privacy staff, or information-security review.

The prototype must not:

- claim institutional approval that has not been documented;
- claim that Gemini searched a library, read licensed full text, or verified holdings;
- fabricate citations, resource names, URLs, staff identities, or credentials;
- store raw research topics, assignment details, or contact information by default;
- accept sensitive health, education-record, confidential, or personally identifying information as an intended use;
- present automated checks as librarian or institutional approval;
- activate an institution profile whose operational values and owners remain unapproved;
- imply that a generated orientation is a research conclusion or exhaustive literature review.

Before an institutional pilot, the institution must approve the data inventory, student notice, provider terms, retention/deletion process, accessibility findings, security test, content owners, hosting/support model, incident response, and release/rollback procedure.
