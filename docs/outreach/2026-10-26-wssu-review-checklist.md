# WSSU review checklist

Suggested text to paste into a follow-up email. This is a draft only; no message has been sent.

---

As promised, here’s a short checklist for your team. This version demonstrates Wake Forest/ZSR resources; a WSSU version would need O’Kelly’s resources, access links, and librarian contacts configured separately.

Please try a research question of your choosing, ask one follow-up to refine it, and open two recommended sources. Rate each area from **1 (poor) to 5 (excellent)**, or mark **not tested**, and add any notes:

1. **Source quality:** Are the sources real, relevant to the complete question, and accurately described? Please flag any incorrect citation or misleading description.
2. **Research guidance:** Does it help narrow the question and suggest useful search terms? Does the follow-up keep your topic and apply the requested change?
3. **Ease of use:** Could a student navigate it with little instruction? Is it clear what happened when a search fails or finds no matches?
4. **Resource discovery:** Are the suggested databases, guides, and other research resources appropriate? What O’Kelly resources would a WSSU version need?
5. **Human support:** Is it easy to find appropriate librarian help? If faculty suggestions appear, are the matches and reasons appropriate?
6. **Overall fit:** Would a WSSU-adapted version be worth piloting with students? What would need to change first?

If possible, include the question and follow-up you tried, one example of a useful or weak result, and your single highest-priority improvement. Please leave out student names and sensitive information.

Thank you—candid feedback, especially examples of where it falls short, will help me improve it.

---

## Reviewer notes

The 1–5 ratings are usability and usefulness feedback, not an objective accuracy rate. Record source existence, topical fit and description correctness separately when auditing results. Do not average a nonexistent citation into an otherwise good overall score. “Not tested” is not zero.

This remains a Wake demonstration. WSSU access, coverage, staff availability, endorsement and deployment are not established by this review. O’Kelly’s public [reference service](https://wssu.edu/academics/cg-okelly-library/library-services/okelly-reference.html) and [library homepage](https://wssu.edu/academics/cg-okelly-library/index.html) are starting points for discussing local research support and resource configuration, checked October 26, 2026.
