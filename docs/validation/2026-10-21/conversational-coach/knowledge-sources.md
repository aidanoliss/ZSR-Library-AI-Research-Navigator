# Research-coach guidance snapshot

Version: `2026-10-21.1`. Selected on October 21, 2026.

These seven cards are developer-curated paraphrases of public ZSR pages. They are not quotations, study findings, an institutional policy interpretation, or evidence of ZSR/librarian approval. They support research-process coaching when a student has not attached articles. They do not authorize factual claims about a student's subject or summaries of unprovided studies.

## Retrieval and curation limits

The citation-tools, systematic-searching, AI-literacy, and librarian-support pages were read through successful public-page opens. The topic-refinement, HST search-techniques, and FYS evaluation pages were read through detailed primary-page text returned by the search tool; subsequent direct opens encountered HTTP 429 or timeouts. The card `retrieval` field records this difference. `checkedAt` is the curation date, not a claim of a live fetch on each conversation turn.

The FYS guide contains course-specific age/page-length examples; these are deliberately omitted so the coach does not impose them as universal scholarly-quality requirements. The HST guide's specific truncation/wildcard examples are not generalized into universal syntax. The systematic-review guide's aspirational completeness is not a guarantee of Navigator coverage. The citation and AI cards include explicit application boundaries so these summaries cannot be mistaken for validation of a generated citation, an instructor's permission, or official library advice.

No paywalled content, student data, personal librarian contact details, live hours, licensed database text, or current subscription promises are included. The snapshot is manually bounded and does not retrieve arbitrary URLs supplied by users or model output. It should be reviewed with ZSR librarians before institutional use and refreshed when the linked guidance changes. A known page URL and a matching card ID establish reference provenance, not semantic correctness of an AI response.
